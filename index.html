Workshop proposal

From Grant Writing to Grant Engineering

AI for Horizon Europe proposals — a hands-on workshop for researchers and research managers

Five practical hours: you will analyse a real Horizon Europe call with ChatGPT, turn evidence into a defensible GO/NO-GO decision, strengthen a concept against the call — and, in groups, assemble an AI-supported proposal workflow with human judgement at every checkpoint.

5 working hours + 2 × 15 min breaks · in person · TU Dresden

The programme follows your brief: practical work over theory, individual and group formats, ChatGPT as the working tool. It builds on trainings delivered for research institutions and European Commission audiences.

Workshop programme

01

Block 1 90 minutes

Read the call systematically — and miss nothing

A Horizon Europe call carries more decisions than text — extract all of them, and check every answer.

−

What your team gains

Two gains in one block: a working command of ChatGPT — what it can do, where it errs, how to guide it — and its first serious application: EU calls analysed systematically and at scale, verified against the source — packaged as a skill you'll reach for every time a new call opens.

Main contents

  • How to set up and personalise ChatGPT and its power functions.
  • Setting up your working Project — and understanding the loop between Chat and Work: Chat brainstorms, Work executes.
  • From 5D prompt to skills: the skill finds, locates and cites — it never decides.

You take home

your own call-analysis skill, built during the session — plus the filled required-facts checklist.

02

Block 2 60 minutes

From idea to evidence: the GO/NO-GO discipline

A weak early fit cannot be written away later — decide where writing time goes before it is spent.

−

What your team gains

An evidence-first decision practice: AI organises what is true about a call, a profile and an idea; you verify, endorse and decide.

Main contents

  • Using AI to organise everything that bears on the decision: the call analysis, the project concept, your organisation's profile and your own thoughts.
  • Building new skills — and learning how the whole process revolves around human judgement: what moves forward, what stops.
  • Designing a small working process of your own: which inputs go in, what comes out, how the steps are packed into skills — and where your judgement decides.

You take home

an evidence-capture routine and a checkpoint discipline your team can repeat.

03

Block 3 45 minutes

Strengthen the concept against the call

Objectives, work plan and argumentation that survive evaluation — fixed before drafting starts.

−

What your team gains

Making a concept that got its GO stronger with AI: weak spots found, drafts improved — and the judgement remains yours. A routine you can repeat on every future concept.

Main contents

  • Learning how to pack a complex piece of work — a full concept review — into a new repeatable skill.
  • Checking the concept against the call: scope, requirements, coherence and workload — question by question.
  • Structure, coherence and clarity passes with ChatGPT — plus the citation discipline that prevents invented sources.

You take home

your own concept-check skill, ready for the next concept — plus the checklist it runs on.

04

Block 4 85 minutes

Group work: assemble your AI-supported workflow

You have checked the concept — now design what comes next.

−

What your team gains

Beyond understanding what AI workflows are: the capability to build one. Every group designs a working piece of the process itself — and leaves knowing how to do it again at home.

Main contents

  • Zooming out to the big picture: where everything we did since the morning sits in the full proposal-development chain — and what comes next.
  • Groups pick the next two or three steps they want to cover — and work them in detail: define the inputs and outputs, develop the skills, and run them to see whether they work.
  • Each group presents its approach — what worked, what didn't, lessons learned — so groups learn from one another and lay the foundations for AI workflows that serve teams, not just individuals.

How we work

This is practical work, built on the key concepts of AI workflow engineering. Demonstrations first, then individual and group work — led by Kristjan Zemljič throughout, with prepared examples so participants with different experience can work at their own pace. Checking results is part of every block, and the day closes with the take-home kit.

We work on real Horizon Europe material and carefully prepared examples. Confidential or unpublished research content is never entered into AI tools.

Materials and tools you keep

Participants take home the skills built during the day — call analysis, evidence capture, concept check — together with the templates and checklists behind them, the presentation, and everything created during the event.

Group results are collected in a shared folder: each contribution carries the workflow design, the skills that run it, usage instructions and authors — available to the teams of all participating universities.
